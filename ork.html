The Genesis

Born in the Ward.

ORK emerged from the intersection of the hospital ward and the code terminal. Founded in Kirkuk, we began by observing the friction between patient anxiety and clinical data loss.

The Problem

Clinical Entropy.

Iraqi healthcare operates under disorder caused by fragile paper records and administrative bottlenecks that devalue the time of both doctor and patient.

The Vision

A Digital Civilization.

To establish a unified digital infrastructure for the Iraqi medical sector. We envision a future where every private clinic operates as a high-performance command center, powered by data integrity and zero-friction access.
